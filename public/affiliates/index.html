Affiliate program

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Promote NexaStore apps with your promo code. You are paid only for confirmed purchases — not for impressions, likes, or follows.

This is an early program. Rates and rules can change. We do not pay for fake or bot traffic.

How it works

  1. Create a NexaStore account and open Profile → Affiliate dashboard (promoters only — not the admin account).
  2. Your status starts as pending until an admin accepts you. Only active codes earn credit.
  3. Get your unique promo code (e.g. PULSE7X).
  4. Share links like https://nexastore-baj.pages.dev/?promo=YOURCODE or tell buyers to enter the code at checkout.
  5. When a paid purchase is confirmed with your code, it is attributed to you in the dashboard.
  6. Payouts are in USDT on Polygon to the wallet you set, after manual review (weekly when volume exists).

Default reward (early)

10% of the app price per confirmed paid sale attributed to your code (paid by NexaPulse from program budget — not taken from the developer’s direct wallet transfer unless stated later).

Example: 2.00 USDT app → 0.20 USDT affiliate credit after confirmation.

Rules

  • No purchased followers, bot spam, or misleading claims.
  • No pretending to be NexaPulse support.
  • Self-purchases may be excluded.
  • We may pause or remove accounts that break rules.
