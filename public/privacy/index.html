Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

What we collect

  • Account email and profile fields you provide (e.g. developer name, payout wallet)
  • App listings, reviews, and support messages you submit
  • Basic technical data needed to run the site (e.g. visit logs for store analytics)
  • On-chain payment data is public on Polygon by nature of the blockchain

How we use it

  • To operate accounts, listings, and downloads
  • To review apps and respond to support
  • To improve the product and prevent abuse

Sharing

We use infrastructure providers (e.g. hosting, database, auth). We do not sell your email list. Wallet addresses you publish as payout destinations are visible in product flows related to payment.

Retention

We keep account and listing data while your account is active and as needed for security and legal reasons.
