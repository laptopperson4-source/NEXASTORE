USDT on Polygon only

Paid apps are priced in USDT. Send on the Polygon network to the address shown at checkout. Other networks or tokens can mean lost funds.

Network gas

Polygon requires a small amount of POL for gas. That fee goes to the network, not to NexaStore as a commission.

Confirmations

We wait for multiple block confirmations before unlocking a purchase so reorgs are less likely to cause double-spend issues.
