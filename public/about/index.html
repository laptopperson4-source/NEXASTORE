About NexaStore

Last updated: 7 September 2026

NexaStore is an early-stage marketplace for independent software. Developers can list apps; buyers can browse, pay where required, and install.

It is operated by NexaPulse Studios (solo founder project). We are not a large company and we do not claim a large existing user base. The store is real software under active development.

Official store address: https://app.nexapulse.pro (NexaPulse Studios).

What we are building

  • A simple catalog for indie apps
  • Developer tools to publish and edit listings
  • Optional paid apps settled in USDT on Polygon

What we are not

  • Not a substitute for Apple App Store or Google Play for most users
  • Not a bank or custodian for every payment (see Payments)
  • Not “guaranteed sales” — discovery starts near zero on a new marketplace
