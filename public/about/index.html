About NexaStore

Indie marketplace. Crypto checkout. Full earnings for developers.

What we are

NexaStore is a marketplace for independent developers to publish tools and apps. Buyers pay with USDT on Polygon. Where we can, payout goes toward the developer’s wallet with no store cut on that direct flow.

We aim for reviews in hours, not weeks — with clear rules and an early-stage product honesty policy.
